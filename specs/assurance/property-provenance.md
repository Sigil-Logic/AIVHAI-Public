**Status**: Living document  
**Version**: 0.4.3

**Project**: AIVHAI

---

Before the NINJA domain model and requirements exist in full, the properties AIVHAI argues about come from the record: what has already been stated, tested, checked or proved for CHERI (any ISA) and RISC-V designs, encodings, models, implementations and compartmentalisation systems.  This document reads that record property by property and maps AIVHAI's assurance case to it.  For every goal of the skeleton ([#226](https://github.com/Sigil-Logic/AIVHAI-Sigil/issues/226)) and every RVY property RP1 to RP5 it answers three questions: **who first stated it, what is the strongest thing anyone has established about it, and over which model.**  Where the answer is "nobody", that is a gap, listed in §5.

The data is [`property-catalogue.jsonl`](property-catalogue.jsonl), one row per property, derived by `scripts/assurance/build-property-catalogue.py` from the census shares of 2026-10-10 and the recorded corrections in [`catalogue-corrections.json`](catalogue-corrections.json).  Every claim below cites the catalogue rows it rests on by row identifier (`P-A1-039`, `A26-058`, `P-D-013`, …).  A reader checks a claim by printing the rows (`build-property-catalogue.py rows`) and opening each source at the section or line the row cites.  The analytical identifiers of this document are `CAN-nn`, so that they cannot be confused with catalogue rows.

How AIVHAI intends to assure these properties, and the new ones it derives, from requirement to design to evidence and across the whole spectrum of techniques from execution to interactive proof, is the subject of [`README.md`](README.md); this document is that plan's starting inventory.  Nothing in this document reports a result of AIVHAI's.  Every "proved" below is someone else's theorem over someone else's model; it becomes evidence for AIVHAI only when the case attaches the artifact at a commit with a rerun, under the checker's rules.

## 1. Method

**Sources.**  The 63 full texts in `docs/bib/` as of [#235](https://github.com/Sigil-Logic/AIVHAI-Sigil/pull/235), read in full by share, the CHERI-MIPS thesis and paper, the Morello and Morello-Cerise proofs and CHERI Concentrate definition by definition; the pinned `cheriot-abstract-spec` (`f754796a`), Guru's `cheriot` (`BoundsMonotonic.v`), `cap_properties.sail`, `CHERICapProps.bsv`, the pinned RVY model, and the CHERIoT RTOS documents; and 41 adjacent works outside the bibliography (share D, rows `P-D-001` to `P-D-041`), found by search and identified by authors, venue and year, with a DOI where one exists.  Every row is a property **as the source states it**, with the source's own form, status and residual assumptions.

**Status.**  *stated*; *tested* (a suite, a boot, differential testing, an FPGA run); *policy-checked* (a link-time audit of a firmware image against a policy, as `cheriot-audit` does); *smt-checked* (`sail -smt` or an equivalent per-function check); *model-checked* (SVA with Jasper or an open flow, bounded or unbounded); *defined* (a definition or model clause in a mechanized artifact: the vocabulary a theorem is stated in, not a result); *proved* (a theorem accepted by a proof assistant, or, where the artifact column says `hand`, a published pencil-and-paper proof); *proved-elsewhere* (a report or informal summary of a proof whose theorem row, if any, is elsewhere: a pointer, not a result); *assumed* (a hypothesis or an admitted lemma the source relies on); *known-hole* (a limitation the source admits); *disputed* (an open review comment on a draft).

**Evidence direction.**  *supports* the property; *counterexample* (a bug found against it, which shows the property was checked and failed somewhere); *context* (a result about a neighbouring artifact, such as a compiler semantics or a method, that informs but does not establish).  §4's tables count the directions separately: **direct support** is a supporting row that is proved, checked or tested; **reported** is a supporting row that is proved elsewhere.

**Mapping.**  Each row names what it bears on in AIVHAI's case: RP1 to RP5; the skeleton's goals G-c (with G-c4, the in-core accelerator's access checks, and G-c5, tag storage) and G-CF (with G-CF2 the Rocq model, G-CF4 the boundary invariants, G-CF6 the IOPMP); an assumption A-1 to A-6; or a threat-model gap.  A note that begins "none" maps to nothing else.  The mapping is the census's first reading, corrected where review found it wrong; every correction is in `catalogue-corrections.json` with its reason.

Three facts fall out before any reading.  **Most of the record is stated, not established**: 214 of 1,295 rows (17%) are proved or machine-checked.  The 149 proved rows come from several proof families: Isabelle (78), Rocq, Coq and Iris (42), unbounded SVA in Jasper (13), HOL4 (6), Guru (6), pencil-and-paper (3) and Lean (1).  Of these, the results at ISA or core scale for a real CHERI design come from four artifacts: the CHERI-MIPS proofs over L3 in Isabelle, the Morello and Morello-Cerise proofs over Sail in Isabelle and Iris, the CHERIoT abstract specification in Rocq, and the CHERIoT-Ibex observational-correctness proof in Jasper.  The rest are encoding lemmas, idealised capability machines, non-CHERI processors and tooling.  **The strongest results are at the ends**, encoding functions (SMT, HOL4, Guru) and whole-core refinement (Jasper); compartment-level results past a domain transition are rare.  **Nothing in the RISC-V CHERI line is proved for CHERI-RISC-V or RVY at sequence level**: the CHERI ISA v9 text cites only the CHERI-MIPS and Morello proofs (`P-B2-069`, proved elsewhere); the RVY specification contains no test, proof or claim of model authority; the pinned RVY model carries no `$property` at all (`P-A2-103`); and the CHERI-RISC-V `$property` checks date from 2021 and have not been re-run against a current Sail.

## 6. What each source leaves out

Condensed from the census shares' own notes; the catalogue's `assumptions` field carries the per-row detail.  These are the limits AIVHAI must not inherit silently.

- **[@Nienhuis-21] and [@NienhuisJoannouBauereissEtAl-20]** (CHERI-MIPS): proofs over the L3 model, not Sail; uniprocessor; architecturally visible capability flow, not information flow.  Compartment isolation holds under set-up hypotheses and up to the first domain transition.  The thesis's Definition A.74 uses `ERETActions`, whose definition is absent from the transliteration; the PDF must be read for it.
- **[@WoodruffJoannouXiaEtAl-19]** (CHERI Concentrate): the HOL4 results are encoding-function properties; the text says "the last two proofs" verify the fast representability check but gives only the first.
- **[@BauereissCampbellSewellEtAl-22]** (Morello): the eight non-goals, which the skeleton carries as A-4 and A-6: ISA not hardware; no GIC; no timing; sequential only; fixed translation; privileged capability creation disabled; no debugger; an SMT oracle for one lemma; the ASL-to-Sail-to-Isabelle translation unverified.
- **[@HammondAlmeidaBauereissEtAl-25]**: the only isolation result in the record that survives a domain transition, at the cost of EL0 only, exceptions terminating, a trusted OS, address translation disabled, and the T-CHERI properties copied by hand from Isabelle into Rocq and assumed there.
- **`cheriot-abstract-spec`**: the instruction level is assumed; the compressed encoding is abstracted away; the switcher contracts have no proof; the main theorem is vacuous on any trace with a call, return or exception; threads must start isolated; `FuncNormal` compared an instruction with itself at `f754796`, corrected at the hand-off commit `2bab36a` (the hand-off tag), and whether the corrected hypothesis is satisfiable is not shown; revocation, allocator, scheduler, DMA and MMIO are absent.  PR [#239](https://github.com/Sigil-Logic/AIVHAI-Sigil/pull/239) records the hand-off state and rebuts D1.
- **`cap_properties.sail`**: per-function checks only; no permissions, no sealing beyond encodability, no tags, no sequences; unmaintained since 2022.
- **`CHERICapProps.bsv`**: bounds only; no harness and no recorded results.
- **CHERIoT** [@AmarAndersonChenEtAl-26; @AmarChenChisnallEtAl-25; @Chisnall-26]: availability is not a general guarantee; stack allocations are not temporally safe; global memory safety is advisory; the allocator is in the TCB and unverified; monotonicity is SMT-checked only as an inductive step; hardware is assumed bug-free and side-channel-free; switcher verification is "underway, early stages"; the policy checks are link-time audits, not proofs.
- **[@PloixArmstrongMelhamEtAl-25]** (CHERIoT-Ibex): the core under a symbolic memory, so tag-storage integrity is an assumption a DMA device could break; single-issue, cache-less, MMU-less; M-type instructions unproved; debug forbidden; the Sail fork and its SystemVerilog translation are trusted; the top-level argument is informal glue over mechanised lemmas.
- **[@JoannouRuggWoodruffEtAl-24]** (TestRIG): refutes, never proves; sees only what RVFI reports; instruction fetch deliberately not exercised.
- **[@ArmstrongBauereissCampbellEtAl-19]** (Sail): validates models, not hardware; translations "validated but not proved".
- **[@AirdAlmataryAmayaGarciaEtAl-26b]** (RVY): no test, proof or claim of model authority; seventeen listings hand-maintained; tag storage EEI-defined; the vector interaction is one page; speculation advisory.
- **The VME texts** [@Killian-25; @RISCVInternational-25c; @RISCVInternational-26a; @RISCVInternational-26b]: no protection or verification content; the fault model of background matrix streams is left open.
- **[@FuchsWoodruffRuggEtAl-23; @FuchsWoodruffRuggEtAl-24]**: contracts in prose, tested on one core; caches, prefetchers, page-table walker and DMA excluded.
- **[@Almatary-26b] and [@Almatary-26a]**: prose and a demo; the calling convention "to be refined later"; five review threads open, including the A-5 precondition.
- **[@GaoBaldwinClarkeEtAl-26] and [@Gao-26]** (CheriBSD c18n): tested ABI hygiene under a trusted toolchain, kernel and runtime; isolation not yet complete.
- **[@MarkettosBaldwinBukinEtAl-20] and [@ChengMarkettosJoannouEtAl-25]**: a stated DMA model and a tested interposer for an accelerator outside the core; no IOPMP, no proof.
- **Adjacent works** (share D): Cerise and Katamaran prove universal contracts over idealised machines or a simplified RISC-V PMP, not a CHERI ISA at 64 bits; VeriCHERI's properties are RTL-level and ISA-model-free; Kami's proofs are over non-CHERI processors; the Lean translation of `sail-riscv` type-checks and proves nothing yet (`P-D-029`); Axiomise's CVA6 claim has no auditable artifact (`P-D-031`); OneSpin's contributed formal work is on the 32-bit CV32E40P, and no CVA6-specific OneSpin result was found (`P-D-032`).

## 7. How to regenerate

```bash
B=scripts/assurance/build-property-catalogue.py
python3 $B build   specs/assurance/census specs/assurance/catalogue-corrections.json specs/assurance/property-catalogue.jsonl
python3 $B stats   specs/assurance/property-catalogue.jsonl        # §2
python3 $B targets specs/assurance/property-catalogue.jsonl        # §4
python3 $B check   specs/assurance/property-catalogue.jsonl specs/assurance/property-provenance.md
python3 $B rows    specs/assurance/property-catalogue.jsonl P-A1-039 A26-058
```

The shares are the census agents' reports, committed under [`census/`](census/) as JSON lines, with their prose notes in its README.  The build fails on any non-JSON line, a row without an identifier, a duplicate identifier, or a correction for an unknown row.  `make check` rebuilds the catalogue and fails if the committed copy differs, runs the builder's regression tests, and runs `check`.  A review that finds a row wrong adds an entry with its reason to `catalogue-corrections.json`; nobody edits the catalogue by hand.  §3 to §5 are this document's reading and are revised by hand; `check` verifies that every row they cite exists.
